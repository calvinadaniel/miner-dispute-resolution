Practice principles

What guides the work

Principle I

Fairness and efficiency

One of the fundamental principles of arbitration is that the parties are seeking a fair and efficient resolution process. An important part of the arbitrator’s role is to conduct that process from filing through the issuance of the award.

Principle II

The process belongs to the parties

The arbitral process belongs to the parties to design and implement. The arbitrator manages the process — permitting agreement where parties can, and making a fair decision where they reach an impasse. That management role is crucial before and during the hearing if fairness and efficiency are to be achieved.
