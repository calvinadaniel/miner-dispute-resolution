Overview

Conflict resolution

Finding a peaceful solution to disagreement — swiftly, efficiently, and with dignity for everyone involved.

Conflicts happen

Workplace conflict does not have to become a crisis.

There’s no denying that conflict resolution at the workplace can be difficult. It often arrives with stress and anxiety. When conflicts arise, it’s important to have a plan for resolving them swiftly and efficiently.

Conflict resolution is the process of finding a peaceful solution to a disagreement between two or more parties. Alternative dispute resolution — mediation and arbitration — keeps many of those solutions outside the courtroom.
