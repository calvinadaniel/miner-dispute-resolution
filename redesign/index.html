Practice principles

Fairness and efficiency — from filing to award.

Principle I

A fair and efficient process

Parties seek resolution that is both fair and efficient. An essential part of the arbitrator’s role is to protect that standard from the moment a case is filed through the issuance of the award.

Principle II

The process belongs to the parties

Parties design and implement the arbitral process. The arbitrator manages it — permitting agreement where possible, and deciding fairly where the parties reach an impasse.
